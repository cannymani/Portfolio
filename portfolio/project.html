Minutes - Fixing the broken Demand Flow

A new approach to Demand Management that turns scattered ideas and conversations into structured, actionable demands.

AI-Enabled

ServiceNow

Demand Management often sits between business ideas and product development, but the journey can become fragmented across conversations, documents, approvals, prioritization, and handoffs. This creates friction for both demand submitters and the teams responsible for evaluating and delivering them.

Minutes explores how AI can bring this fragmented journey together — helping teams capture the intent behind a demand, structure the information, facilitate better discussions, and move validated ideas toward action.

The Challenge

The existing demand journey was designed around processes and handoffs rather than the conversations and decisions that actually shape a demand.

The opportunity was to rethink the experience around how people capture an idea, understand its value, discuss it with stakeholders, make decisions, and move it forward — while using AI to reduce the administrative effort involved in that journey.

My Role

I explored the problem from both a product strategy and UX perspective, mapping the demand lifecycle and identifying opportunities where AI could meaningfully assist users rather than simply add another AI layer.

I translated those opportunities into an end-to-end experience spanning demand capture, AI-assisted structuring, collaboration, evaluation, prioritization, and progression toward delivery.
