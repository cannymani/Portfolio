Process Driven

My UX process is human-centered, evidence-driven, and iterative — designed to move from ambiguity to clarity and from ideas to measurable outcomes. I adapt the process to the problem, combining research, strategy, collaboration, and validation to create experiences that are useful, feasible, scalable, and aligned with business goals.

1. Discover

Understand the business, the users, and the constraints before a single screen is drawn.

2. Define

Synthesise findings into a framed problem, clear success criteria, and a shared point of view.

3. Ideate

Explore options widely and collaboratively, then converge on the concepts worth building.

4. Design

Turn concepts into flows, interfaces, and systems that hold up in the real product.

5. Validate

Test with real users, measure against the criteria, and iterate until the solution holds.

6. Deliver & Measure

Ship with engineering, then track adoption and outcomes to inform the next iteration.
